About Me

Biography

I grew up in an average family where determination mattered more than circumstance. After secondary school, my curiosity led me to coding, starting from basic HTML and CSS tutorials on my mobile phone and learning everything through patience and self-teaching. What began as a small interest soon became a passion. I worked on a couple of projects using tools like React, Node.js, and MongoDB which deepened my understanding of modern development.

Now studying computer science, I continue to push myself toward success. My journey reflects quiet persistence, creativity, and a belief that skill and discipline can build a future far beyond where I started.

My Goals in This Program

  • Deepen my understanding of web accessibility standards and implement them in all projects
  • Collaborate with peers to learn different approaches and perspectives in software development
  • Develop strong testing practices to ensure code quality and reliability

Ultimate Goal: By the end of this program, I want to be confident enough to contribute to open-source projects.

Areas Where I Lack Confidence

  • Backend Architecture: While I'm comfortable with frontend, I sometimes struggle with designing efficient backend systems and database schemas
  • Performance Optimization: I know the basics, but advanced optimization techniques and debugging performance bottlenecks still challenge me
  • System Design: Breaking down large-scale systems and making architectural decisions
  • Public Speaking: Presenting my work and explaining technical concepts

I'm committed to working on these areas through consistent practice, seeking mentorship, and stepping out of my comfort zone regularly.
